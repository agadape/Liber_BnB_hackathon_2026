# Liber interface

The illustrated landing and product pages share Liber's printed-paper identity:
warm paper, deep green actions, Newsreader headings and Bricolage body text.
The three original illustrations stay in use. The BNB accent stays in the logo.

## Page structure

- One header, with the original Demo, QRIS sandbox, BNB invoices and Wallet routes.
- A compact test-environment notice on product pages.
- One heading, a short introduction and the task's main action.
- Optional explanations in disclosures; payment facts stay visible.
- Receipt links use completed public test runs, not invented metrics.

The marketing landing follows Taste Skill v2's editorial redesign guidance.
Product pages use the same visual tokens while preserving their transaction
controls, field order, wallet prompts and pending-operation recovery.
The light paper theme deliberately matches the existing print illustrations.

## Reusable components

PageShell sets the content width and spacing. PageHeading, Card, Button,
ExampleProofs and WorkspaceGuide provide the shared layout vocabulary.
Wallet screens use the same components and a compact bottom navigation.

Surfaces and images have a 16px radius, fields 10px, compact notes 8px, and
actions a pill shape. Use the paper and surface tokens instead of pure white.
Small explanatory text must remain readable; preserve visible keyboard focus.

## Content

Keep primary instructions short. Preserve full addresses, amounts, provider
status, chain information and recovery actions wherever needed for a decision.
Do not hide signing warnings, test-token limitations or key-backup guidance.
Terms and consent copy are not part of a visual rewrite.

Keep the distinction clear: Midtrans confirms the QRIS sandbox payment;
BNB timestamps its receipt hash. Native invoice receipts verify a token transfer.
