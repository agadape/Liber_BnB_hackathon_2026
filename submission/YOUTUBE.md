liber:Ghost Protocol — Phone Off. Permission On. | BNB Hackathon Demo

## Description
The sandwich is ready. Your battery is not.

liber:Ghost Protocol explores a prefunded payment permission that can leave your phone. Prepare online, choose one merchant, amount and expiry, then reserve tokens. Carry the authorization on paper. The named merchant stays online and claims once.

This two-minute English demo shows the live preparation interface, a public 5 MockUSDC payment receipt, recorded replay rejection, and a separate 2 MockUSDC reclaim after expiry.
