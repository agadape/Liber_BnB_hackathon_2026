# Ghost film brief

Audience: BNB hackathon judges and prearranged sandbox merchants.
Action: open the live Ghost workspace and inspect the public proof links.
Format: English narration + English subtitles, 120 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. The user's requested 90–120 second explainer takes precedence over the kit's 15–40 second commercial template. Calm narration, legible UI and evidence matter more than ad-speed cutting.

Original Liber palette: paper `#f5f3e9`, deep green `#063d2c`, green `#0b6b4e`, ink `#101e1a`, gold `#f0b90b`. Newsreader and Bricolage Grotesque. Original merchant/buyer/mascot illustrations remain.

User criteria: “video”, “submission form”, “etc banyak lagi”, followed by “OKKE GAS”. Earlier: “English dengan subtitle English”; preserve previous landing illustrations; make the project solid and compact; humorous relatable story, full natural English.

Facts: buyer prepares online; merchant claims online with named wallet and gas; exact amount and expiry; replay rejected; unused owner reclaim after expiry. BSC Testnet/MockUSDC/no cash value. No whole-device-off physical test, paper test, adoption, audit, IDR settlement or delivery guarantee claimed.

Proof: canonical 5 MockUSDC payment and separate 2 MockUSDC reclaim are public live receipt pages. Recorded hosted UI replay rejection is a different voucher. New preparation captures are a UI-only walkthrough with no new transaction. QR media contains an already redeemed sample.
